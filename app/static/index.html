🏛️ DPDP Act 2023 Statutory Compliance

Digital Personal Data Protection Act 2023 (Act No. 22 of 2023) privacy-preserving engineering principles.

✓ Section 8 Safeguards: Zero Raw Audio Retention Policy

Raw citizen audio waveforms are never saved to disk. Memory buffers are zeroized (shredded) immediately after 192-dim vector projection.

✓ Cancellable Biometrics (Orthonormal Bio-Hashing)

Voice embeddings are projected via a secret salt matrix (z = P_k * x). Even in a total database leak, original vocal cords cannot be inverted.

✓ Revocation & Re-issuance Rights

If a citizen's template is compromised, the operator revokes salt k and issues salt k', producing an orthogonal new template without the citizen needing to change their voice.
